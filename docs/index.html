Poop Games

We all poop. We all go on our phone. Play these games instead of brainrotting. Never any ads. All features 100% free.

Three games. One better break.

Crossword: a daily newspaper-style grid. Word Guess Daily: find the five-letter word in six guesses. Blue means the right spot; raspberry means the letter is elsewhere; dark plum means absent. Galactic Raiders: hold arrows to move and Fire to shoot. Clear waves, dodge shots, and beat your best score.

Create your own game

Describe a 2D or 3D game, preview it, and tap Save to games to add it to your dashboard. Apple sign-in includes 10 free creation/revision credits. Creation requires internet; generated games that use external libraries or assets also need internet to play. Generated games may contact third-party asset or library hosts, which receive ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Your created games are saved to your account and cached on your device. Delete your account from Create a game to remove your account, cloud games, prompts, and credit records from the app database. Provider operational logs and backups follow each provider’s retention policies.

Crossword support

Tap New puzzle to begin. Tap a square to select it; tap again to switch direction. Pinch the grid to zoom. Use Clues to jump to a word and Check to find incorrect letters.

Your puzzles save as you play. Find unfinished and completed puzzles inside Crossword. A new puzzle unlocks at local midnight. You can return to saved puzzles anytime.

Need help? Open an issue.

Crossword content

New puzzles use newspaper-style 15 × 15 grids with 70–82 clues. Previously saved mini puzzles keep their original grids and progress.

Word and clue data includes CC0 content from Open Crossword Bank, combined with our original clues and layouts.

Privacy

The three built-in games work without an account. Creating games requires Sign in with Apple. We store your account identifier, Apple-provided email (which may be a private relay address), credit balance, prompts, and game code in Supabase. Railway runs the backend; prompts and existing game code are sent to Anthropic to generate or revise games. We do not include your account email in generation prompts. You choose whether to send prompts and game code to Anthropic before the first generation, and can withdraw that permission in Create a game. No ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Built-in puzzles and progress stay on your device. A small record in the iOS Keychain remembers your puzzle sequence and last start date to prevent repeats. iOS may retain this record after uninstalling. Saved puzzle progress may be lost if you delete the app; device backups are managed by Apple.

Privacy and support links open in your browser. GitHub hosts this page and handles any information you choose to submit in an issue under its own privacy policy.

The 3,000 included puzzles never cycle back to the start. Daily limits follow your device's local calendar. Progress is not synchronized between devices.
